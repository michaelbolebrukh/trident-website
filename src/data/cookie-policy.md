This Cookie Policy explains how TRIDENT MODULAR HOUSING LTD, trading as Trident Modular, uses cookies and similar technologies on [https://tridentmodular.com/](https://tridentmodular.com/).

## 1. What Cookies Are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your device by a website. Cookies are widely used to make websites work, to remember your preferences, and to provide information to the site's owners.

Similar technologies include tracking pixels, software development kits, and browser storage such as local storage and session storage. Where this policy refers to cookies, it covers those technologies too.

## 2. Our Current Position

This website uses one analytics service and nothing else. It runs no advertising pixels and no other third-party tracking scripts, and our own pages store nothing in your browser to identify you.

### Statcounter

We use [Statcounter](https://statcounter.com/) to count visits and understand how the site is used — which pages are read, how visitors arrive, and where something is not working. We use it to improve the site, not to identify you personally.

Statcounter sets a small number of first-party cookies (such as one that tells a first visit apart from a return visit) and records technical information including your IP address, browser and the pages you view. Statcounter's own handling of this data is described in the [Statcounter privacy policy](https://statcounter.com/about/legal/).

You can opt out of Statcounter across all sites that use it at [statcounter.com/about/set-refusal-cookie/](https://statcounter.com/about/set-refusal-cookie/), or block its cookies in your browser — the site works fully without them.

If we introduce anything further, we will update this policy and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent before any non-essential cookie is set.

## 3. Cookies We May Use in Future

Beyond Statcounter, our Privacy Policy sets out the categories of technology we may use as the website develops. They fall into the following groups.

### Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are required for the website to function, to keep it secure, or to deliver something you have specifically asked for — for example remembering the choices you make in a cookie banner.

Under UK law these do not require consent, because the site cannot provide the service without them.

### Functionality Cookies

These remember choices you make, such as a preferred region or display setting, so the site behaves consistently for you.

### Analytics Cookies

These help us understand how the site is used — which pages are visited, how people arrive, and where something is not working. We would use this to improve the site, not to identify you personally.

### Advertising Cookies

These would be used to measure the performance of our advertising, to build advertising audiences, and to show advertisements more relevant to you on platforms such as Google and Meta.

Analytics and advertising cookies are non-essential. We would not set them without your consent.

## 4. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be set by other organisations whose services appear on our pages — for example an embedded video or map, or an advertising platform.

Where a third party sets a cookie, that organisation is responsible for it and its use is governed by that organisation's own cookie and privacy policies. We would name any such provider in this policy before introducing it.

## 5. How to Control Cookies

If and when non-essential cookies are introduced, you will be able to accept or reject them through a consent banner, and to change your choice at any time.

You can also control cookies through your browser. Most browsers let you:

* see what cookies are stored and delete them individually;
* block third-party cookies;
* block cookies from particular sites;
* block all cookies;
* delete all cookies when you close the browser.

Instructions differ by browser and are available in its help pages or settings. Blocking cookies is your right, but please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may stop parts of a website from working.

You can also opt out of certain advertising tracking through your device settings or through industry tools such as the [Your Online Choices](https://www.youronlinechoices.com/) site.
